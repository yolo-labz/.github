## Colours

The mark uses six fills. Use only these.

| Name | Hex | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| Skin mint | `#baf9b1` | Body, ears, arms, tail |
| White | `#e0e1f3` | Eye whites, fangs, claws |
| Accent purple | `#d389fd` | Pupils and the `>` `<` typing marks |
| Ink charcoal | `#22222e` | Brows, mouth interior, shadows, lower keyboard body |
| Keyboard base | `#363546` | Keyboard base and front edge |
| Keys slate | `#5a596c` | Keyboard keys |

Background plate `#19182a` is not part of the mark. Do not add it to the SVG.

## Clear space

Clear space = the height of one eye (E) on every side.

- In v6, E ≈ 115 units (about 1/10 of the mark width).
- Keep E free around the mark. No text, edges or other graphics inside that margin.

## Minimum size

- Full mark: minimum 96 px wide.
- Under 96 px wide: use the head crop, not the full mark.

## Backgrounds

Approved backgrounds: `#1e1e2e` (dark) and `#ffffff` (white).
Renders: `usage-backgrounds.png` (rsvg-convert, 600 px wide each, from v6).

## Do not

- Recolour outside the palette above.
- Add outlines, shadows or gradients.
- Stretch, skew or rotate the mark.
- Crop the ears.
- Place the mark on a busy picture.
